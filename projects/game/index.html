Ordivon Game

Keep registered product truth separate from Game research, use the cheapest claim-preserving pressure before escalating evidence cost, and let actual product consequences—not Agent sophistication or one observer—decide what deserves promotion.

Ordivon Game separates the registered product from executable research treatments and now has a real autonomous product-pressure loop: cheap scoped falsifiers can change canonical behavior, broader retained standing is revalidated before promotion, and Human evidence is reserved for claims that are actually human-specific rather than used as a universal approval stage.
