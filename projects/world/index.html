Operational capability

Ordivon World

Keep native truth with native owners; retain only the cross-owner relationship whose loss makes external observation, transfer, or consequence unsafe to reconstruct.

The narrow relationship layer between independently authoritative systems. World preserves exact cross-owner consequence and trajectory identity, retained uncertainty, and evidence mapping without becoming the owner of external reality, current Presence, provider state, or domain meaning.

Those results were consumed into the existing 0.4 production boundary rather than expanding it.

What this project owns

Facts, products, or methods maintained here.

  • Provider request/consequence binding and UNKNOWN reconciliation
  • Resource Transfer, Message Delivery, and Entity Migration trajectory identity
  • Narrow provider/World temporal provenance
  • Bounded read-only inspection of retained World commitments

What it leaves elsewhere

Responsibilities and claims outside its boundary.

  • No global external-truth or Presence database
  • No universal provider/capability router
  • No duplicated Runtime, network, provider, or domain ownership
  • Delivery, historical receipt, and structural provenance do not become cognition, currentness, or trust
