Which remaining friction belongs above Runtime rather than becoming another primitive?

Prevent semantic convenience, environment closure, external-effect policy, or cleanup pressure from expanding physical execution authority without a reproduced unreconstructable fact.

All research questionsOpen project ↗

01 / Current position

A hypothesis is not the current judgment.

The dossier preserves the live research position. Dated articles preserve the complete evidence and argument that changed it.

Working hypothesis

Most remaining friction belongs with Harness, Host, Workstation, providers, package managers, or domain owners; Runtime should retain only irreducible physical execution, evidence, and lifecycle facts.

Current judgment

Answered for the current P0–P5, HP0, and C1–C10 evidence horizon. Storage pressure produced an owner/retention correction rather than a generic cleanup service, and later operational-realization pressure kept currentness, retry, evidence scope, identity, authority, retention, and reclamation separable without reopening Runtime Foundations.

02 / Decision boundary

What keeps this Question alive?

Why it matters

Every Runtime primitive becomes permanent compatibility, recovery, and release debt inside the physical authority boundary.

Next admitted test

Keep the graduated boundary closed. Reopen only on a new physical falsifier: a repeated current operation that cannot be recovered, replayed, observed, or explained without one additional Runtime-owned fact under a materially different workload or execution target.

Condition for deletion

A repeated current failure survives owner-local repair and cannot be represented with existing Workspace/Job/Attempt/process/Artifact/source/lifecycle evidence without adding a new durable Runtime fact.
