Can Agent capital judgment remain separate from financial effect authority?

Pressure quantitative research, proposal/decision semantics, signer and credential authority, one-shot dispatch, and venue reconciliation without turning a backtest or model conclusion into permission to trade.

All research questionsOpen project ↗

01 / Current position

A hypothesis is not the current judgment.

The dossier preserves the live research position. Dated articles preserve the complete evidence and argument that changed it.

Working hypothesis

Agent judgment can remain flexible while deterministic Finance/Executor machinery owns exact effect identity, admission, dispatch evidence, and reconciliation under explicit deployment assurance.

Current judgment

Testing at a later boundary than the original QB experiments. Finance now preserves real FinancialResources independently of current risky positions, aggregates component portfolio state with explicit FX/temporal evidence, binds owner-managed capital through replayable CapitalState/Exposure context, and blocks both stale state-changing Decisions and unsupported owner-contribution shortcuts. Context v21 routes only work pulled by current Finance consumers into authoritative obligations while external financial submission remains independently fail-closed.

02 / Decision boundary

What keeps this Question alive?

Why it matters

Real-capital systems fail dangerously when predictive evidence, semantic judgment, API permission, dispatch identity, and final venue consequence are treated as the same state.

Next admitted test

Use the current owner-capital and consumer-bound context to evaluate real opportunities without relabeling missing return/history evidence as zero risk or positive contribution. Keep external submission separate until its selected deployment profile, current credentials/signer, one-shot dispatch, and reconciliation prerequisites are explicitly admitted.

Condition for deletion

A simpler owner-native venue integration preserves the same effect identity, one-shot dispatch, evidence, and reconciliation with less permanent Finance machinery, or the Agent/effect boundary fails under a bounded canary.
