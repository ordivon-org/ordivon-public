What operational surface is required for durable semantic Host continuity?

Determine the smallest inspection, recovery, backup/restore, deployment, and MCP surface needed to operate Host without expanding it into an operations platform.

All research questionsOpen project ↗

01 / Current position

A hypothesis is not the current judgment.

The dossier preserves the live research position. Dated articles preserve the complete evidence and argument that changed it.

Working hypothesis

A small Host-local surface is sufficient when every foreign fact remains an opaque reference that its native owner revalidates.

Current judgment

Answered for the current 0.4.x product scope. Host now has schema-v5 Journal/CAS, full-history validation, Doctor, backup/restore, receipt-bound deployment/rollback, WorkingCheckpoint recovery, and exactly six public MCP Tools. Runtime health, source currentness, Provider state, and domain verification are intentionally not folded into Host operations.

02 / Decision boundary

What keeps this Question alive?

Why it matters

A correct continuity model still needs observable and recoverable product behavior, but operational convenience should not recreate deleted coordination, execution, or cross-owner truth roles.

Next admitted test

Continue ordinary operation and product hardening. Reopen the research question only if a repeated Host-local failure cannot be diagnosed, recovered, or rolled back with the current bounded surface without importing foreign-owner truth.

Condition for deletion

A current Host-owned operational failure repeatedly requires one additional durable Host fact or operation that cannot be reconstructed from Journal/CAS plus the existing inspection/recovery surface.
