How do we know a creative improvement is not just a searched winner or model preference?

Separate richer perception, evidence-grounded meaning, real encounter, typed consequence, search history, and independent holdout before promoting a creative prior.

All research questionsOpen project ↗

01 / Current position

A hypothesis is not the current judgment.

The dossier preserves the live research position. Dated articles preserve the complete evidence and argument that changed it.

Working hypothesis

Exact artifact identity, search-aware evidence, realized encounter, grounded consequence, and independent OOS support can support bounded creative learning without a universal reward model.

Current judgment

Testing after R4–R6. Rich perception earned medium-local equipment, grounding proved more stable than fine labels, and R6 showed a 5,000-candidate zero-effect search could manufacture an impressive winner that failed stronger evidence. One reveal-order effect replicated directionally on pristine content for one Agent-observer class.

02 / Decision boundary

What keeps this Question alive?

Why it matters

Agent-scale generation makes false winners cheap: a system can search until one variant looks exceptional and then mistake selection noise or one observer class for creative truth.

Next admitted test

Change observer class/provider and repeat frozen interventions across new artifact worlds. Introduce human evidence only when making a human comprehension, preference, trust, memory, or behavior claim.

Condition for deletion

A simpler production/evaluation loop reaches the same grounded and OOS conclusions without preserving search history or encounter identity, or the retained creative effect disappears consistently under independent observers and content.
